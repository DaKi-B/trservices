Telephony

We design and implement modern telephone systems for business environments, from small offices to complex multi-site enterprise platforms.

Reliable business communication

Telephone systems are still an important part of everyday business, especially in organisations that need clear internal communication, efficient contact with customers and reliable availability of their teams. TR Services develops telephony solutions that match the client's real needs, with a focus on stability, simple management and long-term sustainability. We work with equipment and platforms from Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise, one of the leading manufacturers in business telephony.

Scope of the solution

What our solution includes

Office telephone systems

Systems for everyday business communication, suited to small and medium-sized organisations.

Enterprise platforms

Solutions for larger and distributed organisations, supporting multiple sites and a large number of users.

Hybrid systems

Connecting IP and SIP technologies with the client's existing telephone infrastructure.

Centralised administration

Managing users, permissions and configuration through a single platform.

Scalable solutions

Systems designed to grow with the organisation, without changing the foundation.

Adapted to different types of buildings

Telephone infrastructure is designed around how the client works and the type of building. Solutions can be used in offices, hotels, healthcare facilities, logistics centres and other organisations where communication is part of daily operations.

Integration with the rest of the system

Modern telephony doesn't work in isolation. That is why TR Services designs telephone systems as part of a wider technology whole, with the option to integrate with the network infrastructure, hotel systems, administrative platforms and other services that matter for the client's efficient operation.
