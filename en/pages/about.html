About us

TR Services is an independent ICT system integrator focused on network infrastructure, communication systems, multimedia solutions and technology platforms for demanding business environments.

A partner for complex technology systems

Our work focuses on the design, implementation and long-term support of integrated technology solutions. Our approach is built on understanding our clients' real operational needs, on stable infrastructure, and on systems that can grow together with the building, the organisation and the business.

Our vision

Our vision is to become a leading partner in information and communication technology, recognised for the quality of our solutions, the expertise of our team and a responsible approach to every project. We stand out particularly in hospitality and healthcare, as well as in critical infrastructure projects, where reliability, continuity and system stability are essential. By integrating modern technologies and following industry developments, we help organisations build a secure, efficient and technologically advanced environment that supports their long-term growth.

Our mission

Our mission is to give every client, regardless of size or industry, a reliable technology infrastructure they can depend on. We design, implement and maintain stable, long-lasting solutions, supporting our partners at every stage of their technological development. We build long-term relationships based on trust, quality of service and the expertise of our team.

History

1919
Téléautomate is founded, a company from which part of the group's history originates.
1984
TR Services is formed through the merger of Téléphonie Européenne and Téléautomate.
1993-2000
A period of growth through changes of ownership, market positioning and acquisitions.
2008
A new TR Services Group is formed following the acquisition of Artelcom Group.
2009
International expansion and the opening of a branch in Belgrade.
Today
TR Services Serbia develops and delivers integrated ICT solutions for a wide range of industries and building types.

People and experience

Many years of experience in telecommunications, network systems and the integration of application and infrastructure platforms have shaped a company that today acts as a trusted technology partner on the local and regional market.
