Hotel solution

An integrated technology solution for hotels that brings network, Wi-Fi, telephony, IPTV and other key systems together into one functional, stable whole.

Technology tailored to the hotel experience

A modern hotel needs reliable infrastructure that supports staff in their daily work, meets guest expectations and integrates a range of technology systems. TR Services develops hotel solutions that cover key operational and guest-facing functions, with a focus on stability, simple management and the quality of the overall experience.

Scope of the solution

What our hotel solution includes

Network infrastructure

The foundation for hotel operations and guest services, designed for reliable everyday use.

Wi-Fi systems

Wireless coverage for guests and staff, adapted to each zone and user density.

Captive portal

Simple guest login and network access management by room and zone.

IPTV platforms

In-room information and entertainment with centralised content management.

Subsystem integration

Connecting telephony and other hotel technology subsystems into one whole.

Supporting the hotel's daily operations

A hotel's technology system has to support every zone and function of the building, from rooms and common areas to reception, administration and technical services. That is why we design the solution around how the hotel really operates, its number of users, the type of property and the level of service expected.

The guest at the centre of the system

In a hotel, technology should be almost invisible, yet fully functional. A stable connection, easy access to services, clearly presented content and reliable communication all have a direct impact on the quality of a guest's stay. That is why we see hotel solutions not only as infrastructure, but as part of the overall guest experience.

Centralisation and long-term sustainability

Integrating different systems into one logical whole makes management easier, gives a clearer overview and simplifies maintenance. Our approach means designing a system that supports the hotel's daily operations today, and also future expansion, changing standards and additional services.
