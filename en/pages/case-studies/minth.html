Minth Serbia

Complete technology infrastructure for Minth Group's new factory in Šabac, from structured cabling and networking to video surveillance, access control and AV equipment for meeting rooms, delivered as a single integrated solution.

Location Šabac, Serbia
Client Minth Group
Sector Automotive industry
Delivery On schedule
The brief

All of the building's technology in one delivery

Minth Group, a global manufacturer of automotive components, opened a new factory in Šabac. The new building needed complete technology infrastructure, from the cabling foundation to the systems used every day.

TRS delivered all systems as a single solution: structured cabling, network infrastructure, video surveillance, access control and AV equipment, coordinating the work on an active construction site and delivering on schedule.

Scope of the solution

Integrated factory infrastructure

All systems were designed and installed as one whole, on a shared cabling and network foundation.

Structured cabling

Complete data and power cabling throughout the building's production and office areas.

Network infrastructure

Switches and Wi-Fi access points on the Huawei platform, for reliable wired and wireless connectivity.

CCTV video surveillance

Hikvision cameras for full coverage of the building and the surrounding site.

Access control

Door and zone access management on the Hikvision ACS platform.

AV equipment

Projectors and displays for meeting rooms, ready for everyday business use.

System integration

All systems brought together in one delivery, with a single point of responsibility for all of the building's technology.

Delivery

System details

Cabling infrastructure, video surveillance and network systems were installed in parallel with construction.

Result

A factory ready to run from day one

Delivered on schedule

The complete technology infrastructure was installed on an active construction site and handed over in line with the factory's opening plan.

A single point of responsibility

All of the building's technology systems were delivered by a single contractor, from cabling to AV equipment.

Coordinated delivery of all systems

Cabling, security systems, network and AV equipment were installed in a coordinated way, without mutual delays or later rework.
