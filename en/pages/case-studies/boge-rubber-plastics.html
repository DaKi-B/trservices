The brief

Complete infrastructure for a new industrial facility

BOGE Rubber & Plastics opened a new manufacturing facility in Niš. The building needed complete technology infrastructure, designed and installed in parallel with construction, within a strictly defined timeline.

TRS delivered all systems as a single whole: structured cabling, uninterruptible power, network infrastructure, access control and video surveillance, coordinating the work on an active construction site.

Scope of the solution

Six systems, one delivery

All of the building's systems were designed and installed as part of one coordinated delivery.

Structured cabling

Complete data and power cabling throughout the building, as the foundation for all other systems.

UPS systems

Uninterruptible power for critical systems, on the industrial Schneider Electric Galaxy platform.

Network infrastructure

Switching and routing on Huawei and Fortinet equipment, for reliable connectivity of production and business systems.

Access control

Door and zone access management across the entire complex, in line with the client's security policies.

CCTV video surveillance

Cameras placed throughout the building and its surroundings, for full coverage of the site.

System integration

All systems brought together in one delivery, with a single point of responsibility for the entire infrastructure.

Delivery

System details

The server room, active network equipment and uninterruptible power were delivered as a single technical whole.

Result

Delivered on time, from a single provider

Delivered during construction

The complete IT and security infrastructure was delivered on schedule, working on an active construction site and coordinating with other contractors.

Protected power for critical systems

Industrial UPS systems provide redundant power protection for all of the building's critical systems.

A single point of responsibility

Cabling, network, UPS, access control and CCTV were all delivered by one contractor, which simplifies both construction and later maintenance.
