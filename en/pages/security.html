Security

We design and implement security systems that protect buildings, people and infrastructure, from access control and video surveillance to network security.

Security as part of the whole system

Modern security systems are not only there to respond to incidents, but to provide everyday control, a clear overview and management of access, spaces and infrastructure. TR Services treats security solutions as part of a wider technology whole, so that systems are reliable, clear and adapted to the client's real operating conditions. We work with equipment from Salto and Fortinet, leading manufacturers in access control and network security.

Scope of the solution

What our security solutions include

Access control

Access management for buildings of every size and purpose, by zone and permission level.

CCTV video surveillance

A clear view of spaces and a record of events, with centralised review of recordings.

Firewall protection

The foundation of network security and control of traffic between zones and services.

Security zones

Access segmentation and organisation of spaces by risk level.

System integration

Connecting security systems with the rest of the building's IT and AV infrastructure.

Adapted to the building and its level of risk

Security requirements vary significantly depending on the type of building and how the space is used. Offices, hotels, healthcare facilities, industrial complexes and control centres all have different priorities, procedures and access levels. That is why we develop every solution around specific operational scenarios and expected risks.

Real-time overview and control

An effective security system has to provide a clear view of events and simple management. Our approach includes choosing platforms that support centralised control, review of recordings, management of users and access points, and better coordination between physical and network security.

Long-term stability and support

Beyond implementation, long-term reliability is an important part of every security system. That is why we design solutions that are stable, easy to maintain and ready for further expansion, backed by support that keeps the system functional and clear in day-to-day use.
