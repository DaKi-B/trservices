Network solutions

We design, implement and optimise network infrastructure for secure, stable and scalable connectivity in business, hotel, healthcare and industrial environments.

The network as the foundation of the whole system

High-quality network infrastructure is the foundation of modern IT, AV and security systems. That is why TR Services takes a systematic approach to network solutions, with a focus on reliability, performance, security and room for future growth. Every project is designed around the client's real needs, the type of building and the expected system load. We work with equipment from Alcatel-Lucent Enterprise and HPE Aruba Networking, leading manufacturers in network infrastructure.

Scope of the solution

What our network solutions include

LAN and WAN infrastructure

Design and implementation of wired network infrastructure connecting sites and systems.

Wi-Fi systems

Wireless coverage for business and public spaces, adapted to user density and the type of space.

Captive portal

Controlled network access for guests and users, with simple login and management.

Network segmentation

Organising traffic by service and zone for security and a clear system overview.

Performance optimisation

Improving the availability, throughput and security of existing network infrastructure.

Adapted to different operational environments

Network infrastructure can't be one-size-fits-all. The requirements of an office, a hotel, a hospital or an industrial plant differ significantly. That is why we adapt network solutions to the specific operating conditions, user density, type of services and expected availability, with particular attention to stability and long-term sustainability.

Integration with other systems

The network is the foundation for telephony, IPTV, digital signage, video walls, access control, CCTV and other technology systems. That is why we design every network solution as part of a wider integrated system, with a clearly defined structure, a clear overview and simpler management and maintenance.

A focus on stability and scalability

A well-designed network has to be ready for both today's and tomorrow's requirements. Our approach includes capacity planning, a clearly organised infrastructure, headroom for expansion and support for future services and additional sites. The result is a network that is not just functional, but a reliable foundation for further business growth.
