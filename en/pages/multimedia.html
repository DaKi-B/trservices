Multimedia

We design and implement multimedia systems that connect content, space and user experience, from IPTV platforms to digital signage and video wall solutions.

Content that works in real spaces

Multimedia systems now play an important role in hotels, office buildings, healthcare facilities, control centres and public spaces. TR Services approaches these solutions as a combination of technology, functionality and user experience, so that content is clear, reliable and easy to manage day to day. We work with equipment and platforms from VITEC, Eona, Samsung and VuWall, leading manufacturers in multimedia systems.

Used in a wide range of environments

Every type of building has different requirements for content, display and management. In hotels the focus is on guest information and a personalised experience, in office buildings on clear communication and presentation, while in control and operations centres the key factors are clarity, reliability and continuous system operation.

Technical and operational integration

We don't treat multimedia systems in isolation, but as part of a wider technology whole. That is why we connect them with the network infrastructure, AV systems, control platforms and other services the building relies on. The result is stable operation, simpler management and a clear logic across the entire system.

A focus on clarity and control

Beyond display quality, how the content is managed matters just as much. Our approach means choosing platforms and architecture that allow centralised control, easy content updates and simple adaptation to different usage scenarios. The result is systems that are not only attractive, but also efficient to operate.
